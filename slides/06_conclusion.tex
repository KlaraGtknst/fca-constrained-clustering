\section{Conclusion}

\begin{frame}{Takeaways}
\begin{itemize}
    \item The BankSearch task starts with a useful but non-intensional manual hierarchy.
    \item \ac{fca} adds intensional descriptions: clusters are explained by shared attributes or topics.
    \item \ac{mlb} constraints let us carry the manual hierarchy into the \ac{fca} framework.
    \item Consistency shows exact expert-data agreement is rare; coherence shows the meaningful partial alignments.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Limitations and Future Work}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Iceberg pruning may remove low-support concepts that could overlap exactly.
    \item The current case study compares two knowledge sources; real workflows may involve more.
    \item Future work: threshold selection for coherence lattices.
    \item Future work: alternative overlap measures beyond Jaccard similarity.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
