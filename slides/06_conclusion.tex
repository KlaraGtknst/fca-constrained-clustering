\section{Conclusion}

\begin{frame}{Takeaways}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \ac{mlb} constraints can be represented natively in \ac{fca} through a closure operator.
    \item The resulting concept lattice provides the valid cluster search space for constrained hierarchies.
    \item Exact consistency between expert-defined and topic-model hierarchies is rare in the BankSearch case study.
    \item Coherence lattices are better suited for analyzing heterogeneous knowledge sources because they capture partial semantic agreement.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Limitations and Future Work}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Iceberg pruning may remove low-support concepts that could overlap exactly.
    \item The current case study compares two knowledge sources; real workflows may involve more.
    \item Future work: threshold selection for coherence lattices.
    \item Future work: alternative overlap measures beyond Jaccard similarity.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
