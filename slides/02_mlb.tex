\begin{frame}{Constraints in FCA}
    \textcolor{red}{TODO: omit?}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
\textbf{Pairwise constraints}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textit{Must-link}: two objects should be grouped together.
    \item \textit{Cannot-link}: two objects should not be grouped together.
    \item Used in constrained clustering and constrained \ac{fca} settings~\cite{wagstaff2001constrained,belohlavek2011selecting,hidayat_constrained_fca_2024}.
\end{itemize}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.46\textwidth}
\textbf{Hierarchical constraints}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textit{Must-Link-Before}: two objects should be merged before either is merged with a third.
    \item Designed for hierarchical clustering, where the level of agreement matters~\cite{bade_hierarchical_2014}.
\end{itemize}
\end{column}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{block}{Why this matters for lattice/context generation}
Pairwise constraints constrain a flat grouping decision; \ac{mlb} constraints constrain which closed document sets are admissible at different hierarchy levels.
\end{block}
