\section{Contribution}

\begin{frame}{Our Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item We prove that \ac{mlb} constraints induce a valid closure operator.
    \item We show that the resulting concept lattice is the search space of clusters consistent with the constraints.
    \item We introduce a comparison and integration method for \ac{mlb}-constrained lattices and topic-model lattices.
    \item We evaluate the approach on the BankSearch dataset and distinguish exact \emph{consistency} from relaxed \emph{coherence}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{frame}
